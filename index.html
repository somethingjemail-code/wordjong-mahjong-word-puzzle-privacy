1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise (GPS) location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • The App uses third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting and, if offered, in-app purchases. These SDKs automatically collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, and data about how you use the App and interact with ads.
  • This data is used to show ads (which may be personalized where you allow it), measure and improve the App, and prevent fraud.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money. However, sharing data with advertising partners for targeted advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under some laws. You can limit ad personalization through your device settings (see sections 9 and 11).
  • You can request deletion of your data by emailing us (see section 12).
  • The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

2. Information you provide

The App has no accounts, sign-in or chat. We only receive information from you if you choose to contact us, for example by email. In that case we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything you choose to include (such as your device's Advertising ID). We use it only to answer you and to handle your request.

3. Information stored on your device

The App saves your level progress, power-up inventory, rewards and settings (such as sound and music volume) on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Some SDKs also store identifiers and settings on your device, including your consent choices.

4. Information collected automatically

When you use the App, we and our partners' SDKs may automatically collect the following information:

  • Device identifiers: the Android Advertising ID (AAID), the App Set ID, the Firebase/installation ID and similar identifiers. On iOS, if offered, the IDFA (only with your permission) and the IDFV. Also identifiers that SDKs generate themselves.
  • Device and network information: device make and model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size and resolution, battery level, memory and storage status, mobile carrier, connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile), user-agent and IP address.
  • Approximate location: country, region or city inferred from your IP address. The App does not request or collect precise GPS location.
  • App usage and gameplay data: install date and source, App version, session starts, length and frequency, levels started, completed and failed, scores, time spent, power-ups and rewards used, settings and other in-game events.
  • Advertising data: which ads were requested, shown, clicked, watched or skipped, the rewards earned from rewarded ads, the ad campaign that led you to install the App, and ad revenue data.
  • Purchase data (if in-app purchases are offered): the item purchased, price, currency, time and transaction ID. Payments are processed by Google Play (or the relevant app store). We never receive or store your card or other payment details.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, error reports, performance data (such as load times and frame rate) and technical logs.
  • Consent data: your privacy and consent choices, and when you made them.

Some of this information may be combined, and some is considered personal data under certain laws (for example, advertising IDs and IP addresses under the GDPR).

5. How we use information

  • To run the App and deliver its features, including saving progress and giving rewards for watching rewarded ads.
  • To show ads, including personalized (interest-based) ads where you allow it, and contextual or non-personalized ads otherwise. Also to limit how often you see the same ad.
  • To measure ad performance and attribute installs to ad campaigns.
  • For analytics: understanding how the App is played so we can balance difficulty, fix problems and improve the game.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud (including ad fraud), abuse and security issues.
  • To process and verify in-app purchases, if offered.
  • To respond to your messages and privacy requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

6. Legal bases for processing (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent: for personalized advertising, and for storing or accessing information on your device where consent is required by law. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract: to provide the App and its features to you.
  • Legitimate interests: for analytics, contextual (non-personalized) advertising, crash reporting, security and fraud prevention, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we must process data to comply with the law.

8. Advertising

The App may show rewarded video ads (which you choose to watch for an in-game reward), interstitial ads and banner ads. Ads are provided by the partners listed above. Ad partners may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads based on your interests, which may include information collected across other apps and websites, to measure ad performance and to limit repetition. Where personalized advertising requires your consent, it is shown only if you have given consent. Otherwise you may see non-personalized or contextual ads. Ads are designed to be appropriate for a general audience.

9. Your choices and consent

The data collection described in section 4 is required for the App to work. The App does not have an in-app setting to turn it off. You can still use the following controls:

  • Consent prompt: where the law requires it (for example, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App may ask for your consent before personal data is used for personalized advertising. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). On iOS, you can control app tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Industry opt-outs: you can use tools such as AppChoices (DAA) and Your Online Choices (EDAA).
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the App. This stops all further collection by the App.

12. Data retention and deletion

How to request deletion of your data

Wherever you live, including in the European Union (GDPR), the UK and California, you can ask us to delete your data. To request deletion of the device identifiers, advertising identifiers and analytics data collected by the App and its third-party SDKs, email us at somethingjemail@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include your device's Advertising ID if you can (found in your device's ad settings). We will process the request, forward it to the relevant partners, and confirm within 30 days. Partners keep data according to their own retention policies. For example, Voodoo states that it deletes user-level data when a deletion request is received, or after 25 months without activity. To request deletion of data held by us or our partners, follow the steps in section 10.
