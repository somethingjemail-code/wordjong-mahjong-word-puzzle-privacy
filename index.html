Privacy Policy

WordJong - Mahjong Word Puzzle · ElmanetoProductions · Effective date: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy explains how ElmanetoProductions ("we", "us") handles information when you play WordJong - Mahjong Word Puzzle (the "App") on Android. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • The App uses third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising and analytics. These SDKs may automatically collect device identifiers and usage data, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your level progress, power-up inventory and settings (such as sound and music volume) on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Information collected automatically by third-party SDKs

To show ads (including optional rewarded ads that give you in-game rewards) and to understand how the game is played so we can improve it, the App includes SDKs provided by our publishing partner Voodoo SAS (the TinySauce SDK and/or the VoodooSauce SDK). These SDKs, and the partner SDKs they include, may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Android Advertising ID, the App Set ID, and similar identifiers;
  • Device and network information: device model, operating system version, language, country/region, screen size, IP address (which may be used to estimate an approximate location) and connection type;
  • App usage and gameplay data: sessions, levels started and completed, time played, in-game events and interactions with ads;
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.

This information is used to:

  • show ads, including personalized ads where you have allowed them, and limit how often you see the same ad;
  • measure ad performance and attribute app installs to ad campaigns;
  • run analytics that help us balance difficulty and improve the game;
  • detect fraud, and keep the App stable and secure.
Versions of the App released before ads and analytics are added may not include these SDKs. This policy describes the App as it is, or will be, distributed with them.

5. Your choices and consent

  • Consent: where required by law (for example, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personal data is used for personalized advertising. You can change your choice later in the App's settings, where available.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the App stops all data collection by the App.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Because we do not run accounts, partner SDKs identify you only by device identifiers. To make a request, contact us at the email below and include your Advertising ID if you can, or contact the relevant partner directly using the links above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Data held by partners is kept according to their own retention policies. For example, Voodoo states that it deletes user-level data when a deletion request is received, or after 25 months without activity.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience word puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help get it deleted.

9. Security

We rely on reputable partners that use industry-standard safeguards, such as encrypted connections, to protect data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

10. International transfers

Our partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and countries in the European Union, with appropriate safeguards where the law requires them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when new features or partners are added. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
